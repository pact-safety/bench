# DS&A fundamentals — external drills

A companion track for un-rusting on data structures and algorithms.
[Structy](https://www.structy.net/problem-index) by Alvin Zablan is a
well-sequenced DS&A course; a large share of its early problems —
Introduction, Big-O, most of Hashing, Beginner Recursion, Linked List I,
Binary Tree I, Graph I, Sliding Window, and Dynamic Programming — are
**free to work through** with video walkthroughs, no subscription.

This track is a **map to that free set**, in the order that builds best.
The bench hosts none of Structy's content — open the source link, work
the problems on structy.net, and mark a section done here once you've
cleared its topic.

**Where this fits:** this is the outbound companion. The bench also ships
its own **originally authored** fundamentals problems covering the same
canonical topics (find them via the `fundamentals` tag) — those work
offline, are self-contained, and are the ones to reach for when you want
to practice inside the bench itself. Use this track when you want Alvin's
video explanations alongside the reps.

All linked content is the author's work and lives on structy.net; the
bench only indexes it as a learning path.
