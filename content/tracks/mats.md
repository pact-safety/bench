# MATS Autumn 2026 — empirical track

Timed reps against the real Stage 2 exam shape. The drill partner picks
(or takes) Mode A or B, runs the rep on a visible clock, scores what the
assessment actually scores — reasoning transparency in A, engineering
judgment in B — and reports your **trend across reps**.

Each rep opens with `=== REP N · MODE X · target M min ===` and closes
with an elapsed reading, so the bench can chart pace over time.
