Signal

Readiness answers whether you can do the work. This answers the question a selection process actually reads: is there anything a stranger can look at that shows it — and what happened when you applied.

These criteria come from guidance programs have published, not from guesses about what they secretly want. Meeting them does not produce an acceptance: these rounds are capacity-bound and single-digit-percent. What they do is make work you have already done legible, and separate the strong version of a line on your CV from the weak one.
