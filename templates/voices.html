How this works

The selected voice's persona is prepended to the review system prompt — the structured per-phase output, the anti-sycophancy rules, and the rubric all remain. What changes is the emphasis: Hamming will flag courage gaps, Petroski will name failure modes, Meadows will push you toward higher leverage points. Same problem, different lens.

Adding more voices is a 12-line append in app/services/voices.py — open an issue or send a PR with a thinker whose lens you want.
