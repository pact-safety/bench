How this works

For each assessed axis the JD weights, we compare the assessment score with the inferred JD weight and convert any gap to illustrative hours using the fixed rate above. The slider divides those hours by a weekly schedule. JD weights describe emphasis, not validated proficiency thresholds.

Unassessed axes and axes without a positive JD weight are excluded. These numbers do not establish whether you are ready for an interview, or how long it will take to learn the material. Retake the assessment to record a new snapshot.
