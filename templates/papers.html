What each pass is for

  • 1 — Orient. Title, abstract, figures. Decide if it is relevant.
  • 2 — Claim. Intro, conclusion, figures again, scan the rest.
  • 3 — Mechanism. The whole paper, skipping maths you do not know yet.
  • 4 — Depth. The maths and the terms you skipped. Only for papers you will build on or argue with.
